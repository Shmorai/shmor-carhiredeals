How CarHireDeals works

Getting a like-for-like comparison

Check that each site shows the same car class (for example “compact” or “intermediate SUV”), the same pick-up location and the same times. Then compare the final total, including taxes and fees, and the insurance excess, fuel policy and mileage limit. A slightly higher price with a lower excess or a free cancellation policy can be the better deal.

Brokers and rental brands that don’t accept pre-filled searches open on their own search page, where you just type your trip again.

Tips that often save money

  • Try a local depot as well as the airport: airport locations often add fees.
  • Shift your pick-up or drop-off time: rentals are usually priced in 24-hour periods, so returning an hour late can add a whole day.
  • Log in to brand loyalty programmes: several show member rates or skip-the-counter pick-up.
  • Book a free-cancellation rate early and check again nearer the date.

Frequently asked questions

Why do only some sites open with my trip filled in?
KAYAK and Expedia accept a pick-up place and dates in their search links. Other sites need their own location codes, so they open on their search page instead.
What does “Sites in” change?
It picks the version of each site for your country, such as kayak.com for the USA, kayak.co.uk for the UK or kayak.ie for Europe, so prices show in dollars, pounds or euros.
Do you keep my search details?
Your search is handled in your browser and passed to the site you open. CarHireDeals doesn’t need an account or personal details. See the Shmor.ai privacy policy for more.
