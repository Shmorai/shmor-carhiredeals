Austria's digital vignette for 2027: what hire car drivers need to know

If a trip takes you onto Austria's motorways, you need a vignette, and from the 2027 vignette year it will be digital only. That matters if you hire a car: you need to know whether yours already has one. Here is what the Austrian government says, as of November 2026.

What is changing with the Austrian vignette?

Austria charges a time-based toll on its motorways and expressways for cars up to 3.5 tonnes. According to the Austrian government's official portal, the vignette is currently sold as a sticker or a digital vignette, but for the 2027 vignette year it will only be available as a digital vignette. A digital vignette is linked to the car's licence plate number rather than to a sticker on the windscreen.

Does a hire car already have a vignette?

The official page doesn't say anything about rented cars, so don't assume. In practice, it depends on the rental company and the country where you pick up the car, so ask before you leave the desk: does the car have a valid Austrian vignette, and for how long? If you pick up in Austria, check the booking terms; if you pick up in a neighbouring country and drive in, you may need to buy one yourself. See our guide to French emission stickers for a similar check in France, and our guide to driving licences abroad.

Austrian vignette prices and types for cars

The Austrian government lists these prices for cars for 2026 (valid from 1 December 2025): 1-day €9.60 (digital only), 10-day €12.80, 2-month €32.00, and annual €106.80. Prices for 2027 may differ, so check ASFINAG before you buy. For a short trip, the 10-day vignette is usually the one that fits a hire car.

How and when to buy

  • Where: the ASFINAG webshop and app, authorised online partners, ASFINAG toll stations and vending machines, motoring clubs such as ADAC, ARBÖ and ÖAMTC, and selected petrol stations and tobacconists.
  • Start date: the official page says online 10-day and 1-day digital vignettes are valid immediately, while online annual and 2-month ones for consumers only become valid from the 18th day after purchase.
  • Check the plate: a wrongly registered plate number counts as an administrative offence, so enter the registration exactly as it appears on the hire car's documents.

What happens if you drive without one?

The vignette must be validly paid before you use the toll roads: buying one afterwards doesn't count. Drivers caught without a valid vignette must pay a substitute toll, and if they don't, administrative penalties of €300 to €3,000 can apply, per the Austrian government. Frequently asked questions

Is the Austrian toll sticker being abolished?
According to the Austrian government portal, the vignette will only be available as a digital vignette for the 2027 vignette year. Stickers are no longer sold for that year.
Do I need a vignette to drive a hire car in Austria?
Yes, to use Austrian motorways and expressways, any car up to 3.5 tonnes needs a valid vignette. Whether your hire car already has one depends on the rental company, so ask when you pick it up.
What is the fine for no vignette in Austria?
Drivers without a valid vignette pay a substitute toll; if unpaid, administrative penalties of €300 to €3,000 can apply, according to the Austrian government.
