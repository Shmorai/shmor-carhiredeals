Dartford Crossing charge in a hire car: how to pay the Dart Charge

There is no toll booth at the Dartford Crossing east of London, and that catches out many hire car drivers, especially after landing at Gatwick, Heathrow or Stansted. Here is what GOV.UK says about when the charge applies and how to pay, as of November 2026, plus what to check in your rental agreement.

How the Dartford Crossing charge works

The Dart Charge is paid online or by other remote methods rather than at a booth. GOV.UK says the charge usually applies between 6am and 10pm, including weekends and bank holidays. Motorcycles, mopeds and quad bikes do not need to pay. Because the charge is linked to the vehicle’s registration, you will need the number plate of your hire car.

The deadline: pay by midnight the day after

GOV.UK says you must pay by midnight the day after you cross, and late payment may lead to a fine. You can also pay in advance. A one-off payment for future crossings is valid for 12 months and is used automatically on your next crossing, and pre-pay accounts get a discount of up to 20% according to GOV.UK. For a short rental, a pay-per-crossing payment is usually the simplest, but check the official page for the current charge for your vehicle class.

Hire car tips: what to check with your rental company

The registration belongs to the rental company, but the driver on the agreement is usually the one who will be asked for road charges, so read your rental agreement to see what happens if a charge goes unpaid. Practical steps:

  • Note the number plate when you collect the car and keep it handy.
  • Pay online as soon as you have crossed, rather than waiting for the deadline.
  • Ask the desk whether the company has its own toll or charge service, and what it costs.
  • Keep your payment confirmation until your rental deposit is released.

Other UK charges to plan for

Different cities have their own schemes, such as London’s congestion and low-emission zone charges, so check each before you drive in. Frequently asked questions

When do I have to pay the Dartford Crossing charge?
GOV.UK says to pay by midnight the day after you cross, or pay in advance. Late payment may lead to a fine.
Is there a toll booth at the Dartford Crossing?
No. The charge is paid remotely, online or by other methods, and is linked to the vehicle’s registration number.
Who pays if I cross in a hire car?
Your rental agreement sets out responsibility for road charges. Read it before you drive and ask the rental company if anything is unclear.
