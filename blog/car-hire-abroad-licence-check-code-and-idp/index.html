Hiring a car abroad: licence check code, IDP and what to bring

A hire car is only yours if the desk accepts your licence. For UK drivers that can mean a DVLA check code, and in some countries an international driving permit. Here’s what the official guidance says.

What to take to the car hire desk

GOV.UK says you need to take your Great Britain or Northern Ireland driving licence with you to drive abroad, and to check it’s still valid. If it’s lost, stolen or damaged you’ll need a replacement before you travel. Your hire company may also ask to see your driving licence information when you pick up the car. Each company sets its own conditions, so check your booking terms; our rental company guide is a starting point for comparing brands.

The licence check code: how it works

If you have a licence issued in England, Wales or Scotland, you can share your driving record with a hire company by creating a licence check code on GOV.UK. According to GOV.UK (as of November 2026):

  • You’ll need your driving licence number, your National Insurance number and the postcode on your licence.
  • The code is valid for 21 days.
  • You can generate it up to 21 days before your trip.

Northern Ireland licences are handled by nidirect. Never share a code with anyone other than the hire company that needs it.

Do you need an international driving permit (IDP)?

It depends on where you’re going, how long you’re staying and what type of licence you hold. GOV.UK’s guidance says:

  • You do not need an IDP to drive in the EU, Switzerland, Norway, Iceland or Liechtenstein with a UK-issued photocard licence.
  • You might need one in some EU countries and Norway if you hold a paper licence or a licence issued in Gibraltar, Guernsey, Jersey or the Isle of Man.
  • In some countries the permit is needed only for stays beyond a set period. For example, GOV.UK lists up to 6 months in Germany and up to 185 days in Portugal for photocard licence holders without an IDP.
  • A few countries say an IDP is needed for car hire itself; GOV.UK’s table lists Bahrain and Botswana, for example.

Always check the country entry on GOV.UK and what your hire company says. If you’re comparing airport car hire, the pick-up country’s rules apply.

Hiring a car in the USA with a UK licence

GOV.UK says the type of IDP depends on the country, and lists the United States as needing a 1949-type permit. It advises you check whether the individual state authority says you need one and whether the hire company does. It adds that you may need both your driving licence and an IDP to hire a car in the US, and that holders of a paper UK licence should take another form of photographic ID such as a passport. Rental companies have their own age and licence rules, so read our young driver guide if you’re under 25.

How to get an IDP

GOV.UK says an IDP costs £5.50 and you can get one in person from certain shops that have PayPoint. You must live in Great Britain or Northern Ireland, have a full UK driving licence and be 18 or over. A 1926 or 1949 permit lasts 12 months; a 1968 permit lasts 3 years or until your UK licence expires, whichever comes first. If you’re travelling through more than one country you might need more than one type. Apply early if you can.

Visiting the UK or Europe from the US

If you’re a US driver hiring a car in Britain, GOV.UK says visitors with a non-GB licence can usually drive in Great Britain for up to 12 months from when they last entered; use GOV.UK’s “driving in Great Britain on a non-GB licence” tool for your licence type. For other countries, check that country’s rules and your rental company’s terms before you travel.

Equipment and insurance

GOV.UK says that if you’re hiring a car, it’s your responsibility to check you have the equipment you need, such as a reflective jacket and warning triangle in many countries, and emission stickers in some European cities, which you may need to buy weeks ahead. It also says insurance is included with a hire car, but you should check what you’re covered for with the hire company. For excess cover, read our excess insurance guide.

Frequently asked questions

How long is a DVLA licence check code valid?
GOV.UK says the check code is valid for 21 days, and you can generate it up to 21 days before your trip. You need your licence number, National Insurance number and the postcode on your licence.
Do I need an IDP to hire a car in France or Italy with a UK licence?
GOV.UK says you do not need an IDP in France or Italy if you hold a UK photocard licence, but you should check whether your hire company says you need one. Paper licence holders should update to a photocard or get an IDP.
Do I need an IDP to hire a car in the USA?
GOV.UK says you may need both your licence and an IDP to hire a car in the US, and to check the state authority and hire company. Paper licence holders should carry another photo ID.
