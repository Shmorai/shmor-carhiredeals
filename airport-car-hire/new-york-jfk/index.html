Picking up your car at JFK

Rental cars at JFK are collected at Federal Circle. Take the AirTrain JFK from your terminal to the Federal Circle station; travel on the AirTrain between the terminals and Federal Circle is free. The rental companies’ counters and cars are there.

Driving in and around New York

Many bridges and tunnels around New York City charge tolls, and Manhattan’s congestion relief zone charges vehicles entering the area south of 60th Street. If you’re staying in Manhattan, it’s often easier to pick up the car on the day you leave the city. Long Island, the Hamptons, the Hudson Valley and the Catskills are all popular drives.

Frequently asked questions

How do I get to the rental cars at JFK?
Take the AirTrain JFK to the Federal Circle station, where the rental companies are based. The AirTrain is free between the terminals and Federal Circle.
Can drivers under 25 rent a car in New York?
New York State law requires rental companies to rent to drivers aged 18 and over who meet their other conditions, but young drivers usually pay a surcharge. See our young driver guide.
